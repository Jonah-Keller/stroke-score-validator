Validate the pre-thrombectomy stroke risk score on your own data

A single-file tool that scores your cohort against the published pre-operative and peri-procedural risk models. It runs entirely on your own computer — your data is never uploaded or transferred.

Download the validator
  1. Download the file above and double-click to open it (runs locally in your browser; works offline).
  2. Load your spreadsheet and confirm the column matches.
  3. Review the results, then click Researcher export (de-identified) to save a summary file.
  4. That file contains no PHI — only scores and outcomes — and can be shared for pooled validation.
