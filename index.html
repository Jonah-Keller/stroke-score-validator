Validating on your own cohort

It takes a few minutes:

  1. Download the validator.
  2. Open it — it runs in your browser, offline.
  3. Load your spreadsheet.
  4. Confirm the column matches.
  5. Click Researcher export (de-identified).

The export contains only computed scores, dichotomized outcomes and aggregate cohort characteristics — no PHI — and can be shared for pooled validation.

Research tool; not for sole clinical decision-making. Patient data is read and processed only in your browser; nothing leaves your machine.
