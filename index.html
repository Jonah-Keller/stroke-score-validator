Validate the published pre-operative / peri-procedural mRS risk scores on your own cohort. Runs entirely on your computer.

✓ RUNS 100% LOCALLY — NO DATA LEAVES YOUR MACHINE

Recommended: download & run offline (for PHI)

⬇ Download the validator (one file)
  1. Download the file above (a single validator.html, ~40 KB).
  2. Double-click it to open in your browser — it runs from your disk, offline. (You can disconnect from the internet.)
  3. Load your CSV/Excel and map the columns; it scores and validates instantly.
  4. Click “⬇ Researcher export (de-identified)” and send me the resulting validation_export.json — it contains no PHI (only score + outcome per patient, plus summary metrics).

Privacy: your patient data is read and processed in your browser only — it is never uploaded anywhere. Safe to use fully offline / air-gapped. Want to verify? Open the file in a text editor and search for fetch — there are no network calls.
