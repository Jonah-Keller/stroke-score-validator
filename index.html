Thrombectomy Outcome Scores

Bedside estimates of 90-day functional independence (mRS 0–2) for patients undergoing mechanical thrombectomy. Choose the score that fits where you are in the patient's course.

Before the procedure: ASCEND →Six variables available during the consent conversation. After the procedure: CAPTaN →Five variables, including passes and mTICI, for counseling the moment the procedure ends. Validate on your data →Download a single-file tool that scores your whole cohort (both scores) locally. Nothing is uploaded.

ASCEND (pre-procedure)

ASCEND = 2×NIHSS + 22×Cancer + 7×Diabetes + 7×Non-ambulatory + 6×Pre-stroke mRS + 2×Smoking  ·  ≥ 42 = higher risk

Validating on your own cohort takes a few minutes: download the validator, open it (it runs in your browser, offline), load your spreadsheet, confirm the column matches, then click Researcher export (de-identified). The export contains only computed scores, dichotomized outcomes and aggregate cohort characteristics — no PHI — and can be shared for pooled validation.

Research tool; not for sole clinical decision-making. Patient data is read and processed only in your browser; nothing leaves your machine. Source: github.com/Jonah-Keller/stroke-score-validator
