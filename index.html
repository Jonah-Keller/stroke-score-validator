ASCEND Score

Predict 90-day functional independence before mechanical thrombectomy, from six variables you already have during the consent conversation.

Validating on your own cohort takes a few minutes: download the validator, open it (it runs in your browser, offline), load your spreadsheet, confirm the column matches, then click Researcher export (de-identified). The export contains only computed scores and dichotomized outcomes — no PHI — and can be shared for pooled validation.

Research tool; not for sole clinical decision-making. Patient data is read and processed only in your browser; nothing leaves your machine. Source: github.com/Jonah-Keller/stroke-score-validator
