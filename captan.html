CAPTaN Score Post-thrombectomy outcome calculator · 90-day functional independence (mRS 0–2)

Five variables documented in every procedural note — Cancer history, Age, Passes, TICI grade, and NIHSS — available the moment the procedure ends. This page does the arithmetic and looks up the probability for you.

Prognostic estimate only. It informs, but does not replace, clinical judgment, and is not a directive for treatment or goals-of-care decisions. Probabilities are calibrated to the derivation cohort (36.6% functional independence); centers with a materially different baseline outcome rate should interpret absolute probabilities accordingly.

The equation

CAPTaN = 22×Cancer + 1×Age + 2×Passes + 2×NIHSS − 7×mTICI

mTICI is scored 0–5 (0, 1, 2a→2, 2b→3, 2c→4, 3→5). Score ≥ 86 flags higher risk of a poor outcome (mRS 3–6).
