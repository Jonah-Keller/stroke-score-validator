Prognostic estimate only. It informs, but does not replace, clinical judgment, and is not a directive to perform or withhold thrombectomy. Probabilities are calibrated to the derivation cohort (36.6% functional independence); centers with a materially different baseline outcome rate should interpret absolute probabilities accordingly.

The equation

ASCEND = 2×NIHSS + 22×Cancer + 7×Diabetes + 7×Non-ambulatory + 6×Pre-stroke mRS + 2×Smoking

Score ≥ 42 flags higher risk of a poor outcome (mRS 3–6). Smoking: current = 1, former = 2 (2 points per level).
